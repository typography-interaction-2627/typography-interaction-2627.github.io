## Brief Demo Time

We want to pick up from [last week’s demo](11.md#and-a-quick-demo), quickly showing how you will structure your final projects:
<!-- .balance -->

1. We’re going to use [cascade layers](../topic/css/index.md#4-external-with-import-to-assign-layer) to avoid any *specificity* problems!

	<sub>These are a somewhat recent addition to CSS, and will help us manage any overlapping rules!</sub>

	* Add this to your landing page `<head>`, replacing our previous two `<link>` elements:

		```html
		<style>
			@import 'reset.css' layer(reset);
			@import 'common.css' layer(common);
		</style>
		```

	* This still “links” your stylesheets (now via [`@import`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@import)), but enforces the exact override order declared by [`@layer`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@layer).

	* Replace the `<link>` elements in your direction pages too, updating the path and the page-specific styles:

		```html
		<style>
			@import '../reset.css' layer(reset);
			@import '../common.css' layer(common);
			@import 'style.css' layer(page);
		</style>
		```

	* Now any styles you write in the page-specific `style.css` will override the `common.css`—no matter the how complicated the selector or [specificity](../topic/css/index.md#specificity)!

1. Then we’ll set up for our three articles. Duplicate your “winning” direction’s directory, `direction-1/` <span class="rarr">→</span> `article-name/`.

	* Duplicate `article-name/` twice for your other texts, `second-one/` and `third-text/`.

	* Update the `<title>` and `<h1>` in each of these to match your different texts.

		<sub>For next week, you will be incorporating the semantic DOM for the *actual* texts!</sub>

	* Update the links on your landing page navigation to point to these new folders (instead of your directions).

	* And likewise update the per-page navigation, so they all link to one-another!

	* “Promote” your winning direction’s styles up to your `common.css`! These are now the basis for your design system.

1. Later (when you don’t need anything from them), you can delete your other directions to “[tidy up](11.md#project-2-recap:~:text=Refactoring%C2%A0/%E2%80%8B%20%E2%80%9CTidying%20Up%E2%80%9D)”!
