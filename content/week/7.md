## Project 1 Recap

Everyone should have received their individual [*Manuscript*](../project/1.md) feedback and evaluations on Slack. Some through-lines:
<!-- .balance -->

- **Attributions?**

	- A number of folks brought in other material that we didn’t discuss in class—whether from previous knowledge, peers, MDN, who knows—and we didn’t see much at all in the way of [attributions](../syllabus.md#attribution).

	- We think we’ve been clear on this—you ***must*** **note this in a comment** and explain the usage and your understanding.

	- In particular, we did not see *any* links to LLM “conversations,” despite [our guidelines](../syllabus.md#our-ai-guidelines) for their use. “Got this from Chat” is not an acceptable attribution! It does not tell us anything about your *understanding*.

- **Better Code “Hygiene”**

	- Commit *smaller* sets of changes, much more often, and with more useful messages. It shows us how (and that) you are working through things, while making your code more resilient to errors. It also gets you in an iterative, incremental thinking/working mode!

	- Likewise, [comment](../topic/html/index.md#comments) your code! We didn’t see nearly enough of this, attribution or otherwise. Tell us/Future You what this means, or does.

	- Proper READMEs! Clearly a lot of folks forgot about this until the last minute (or entirely). We want more attention for these—it is a chance to practice writing (thinking) about your work.

- **A *Systematic* Implementation**

	- Following from above: in code, any time you’re repeating yourself there is probably a better way! [D.R.Y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_repeat_yourself)!

	- In CSS, duplicate declarations might mean you can use [grouped selectors](../topic/css/index.md#compound-and-lists-selectorselector-selector-selector), or maybe reuse [a class](../topic/css/index.md#2-a-class-class-name).

	- But even *better*: could we identify *relationships* with [`calc()`](../topic/box-model/index.md#combined-via-calc), define them as a [`--variable`](../topic/box-model/index.md#defined-as---variable), and use [nesting](../topic/css/index.md#also-native-nesting) to show our *intent* more clearly? Probably!

	- This means leaving time for [*refactoring*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_refactoring) your code—going *back* across your work to find/consolidate/tidy and make your decisions clear. This also necessarily brings cohesion and rhythm to your work!

	- Said another way: we don’t want to see any more “[magic numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_number_(programming)).” Practice unpacking your intuitions!

- **Readability Fundamentals**

	- We had lots of expressive type work! But in this, we often lost sight of [the basics](../topic/typography/index.md#type-size-weight-and-color)—many projects *still* had base paragraph text that was uncomfortable to read—lacking contrast, too-big or too-small, too-short or too-long lines.

	- Put yourself in the mind of your audience: is this comfortable to read? The easiest way to do this: actually *read* your page, as it is designed! Very few did this.

- **An Attention to Detail**

	- In a movie, you don’t want to see a reflection of the cameraman in a mirror or a boom mic in frame. In design, you don’t want to see typos or misused punctuation. It just takes us out of the work, and doesn’t demonstrate *care*.

	- Said another way: as a designer, *you* are the attention to detail. That’s what we do here!

	- This goes beyond our work itself into our communication/instructions, too! If we write it, you can safely assume we mean for you to read (and consider) it.

- **Slack Habits**

	- Always include a link to your updated repo and as *specific* a question(s) as possible—showing us you’ve thought things through first, before asking. Use [threads](https://slack.com/help/articles/115000769927-Use-threads-to-organize-discussions) for this, to keep our main channel understandable.

	- We’ve never heard from about half of you in the chat! We’d like to see more activity overall, and engagement with *each other* there too. Everyone is going through the same stuff! And it’ll only be getting more challenging.

	- Last: when we [ask for acknowledgement](https://mpscd.slack.com/archives/C0BRNBPC6QP/p1790457709940359) that you’ve read something, we expect acknowledgement! It is on you to mind your notifications and stay on top of our communications.
